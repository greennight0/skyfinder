You are a senior Android Engineer, AR Engineer, and Astronomy Software Engineer.

Your task is to build a complete Android application named:

SkyFinder

The application should be production-quality, modular, and easy to maintain.

========================================================
PROJECT GOAL
========================================================

The app has only ONE feature.

When the user opens the camera and points the phone toward the night sky, the app must identify the stars currently visible in that direction and display their names as an augmented reality overlay.

The app DOES NOT use image recognition AI.

Instead it calculates star positions using:

• GPS location
• Current UTC time
• Magnetometer
• Accelerometer
• Gyroscope

Then projects stars into the camera coordinate system.

This is similar to Sky Map or Stellarium Mobile.

Everything should work offline.
